Strength · Upper body

A big horizontal push like the bench press, paired with a pull. Then grip, biceps and triceps. Controlled tempo, clean technique.

Perform · Lower body

A lunge or hinge for strong hips, glutes and legs, then a lower-body conditioning piece with dumbbells, kettlebells and wall balls.

Condition · Engine day

Mobility first, then the main conditioning session on the ski, row and bike ergs, Echo bikes and runs.

Squat day. Paired with hamstring and adductor work for a complete lower body.

Condition · Team day

A team workout using the whole floor and a block run. Hard work, a lot of laughs, the best start to a weekend.

Start

We measure strength, fitness, mobility, power and body composition, so you know your starting point.

Weeks 1 to 3

Technique and a strong movement base. Slower tempo lifting and Zone 2 conditioning.

Weeks 4 to 6

Build lean muscle in the 6 to 10 rep range. Threshold and VO2 max work begins.

Weeks 7 to 9

Fewer reps, heavier loads. 3 to 5 reps on your key lifts, and harder repeat efforts.

Weeks 10 to 12

Turn strength into speed. Moderate loads moved fast, for real-world movement.

Finish

Repeat every test, see how far you have come, and set up the next block.

Questions

I’m not super fit. Can I still do classes?
Absolutely. Classes are designed for all fitness levels. Coaches guide you through options that suit your ability, so you progress at your own pace.
How often should I do classes?
For the best results we recommend 3 or more sessions a week.
What classes do you run?
Coached 50 minute group fitness classes built on the Hybrid Longevity Model, in three styles: Strength, Perform and Condition.
Can I book a class if I’m not a member?
Yes. Pick a class on the timetable and tap Book. You pay the $28 casual price online when you book.
